FAQ

How much smaller will my video get?

It depends on the original file and the compression level you choose — higher compression means a smaller file but lower visual quality.

Why does this take longer than other tools?

Compression now runs on our own server with a real video engine, which is much faster than doing it in a browser. It still takes real time for larger or higher-resolution videos, since every frame is genuinely re-encoded. Speed and resolution optimizations are applied automatically.

Does this work on Mac, Windows, or my phone?

Yes — it runs in any modern browser on Mac, Windows, Chromebook, iPhone, or Android. No app or software to install.
