Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

In short: Your files are processed entirely inside your own browser. They are never uploaded to, transmitted to, or stored on any server. The data described below relates only to advertising, standard website hosting, and a small number of third-party services used by specific tools (detailed below).

1. Who we are

ConvertKoro ("we," "us," or "the Service") operates a growing set of free PDF, image, developer, calculator, audio, and video tools, currently hosted at convertkoro.com. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use the Service and how it is handled.

2. Your files are not collected

Every conversion, compression, editing, and calculation tool on ConvertKoro runs locally in your web browser using client-side code. This means:

  • Your files never leave your device.
  • We do not upload, receive, view, store, or retain any documents, images, audio, or video you process.
  • Once you close or reload the page, any files held in browser memory are discarded.

Because of this design, we have no access to the contents of your files at any point. A small number of tools (OCR and video tools) download a language file or a processing engine from a public CDN the first time you use them; this download contains no data about you or your file, and no part of your file is sent along with it.

3. Information collected automatically

While the tools themselves do not collect your files, some standard information is collected automatically when you visit the site, primarily to display advertising, understand site usage, and operate and secure the website:

  • Advertising data — through Google AdSense (see Section 5), including cookies and similar identifiers, your approximate location, device and browser information, and how you interact with ads.
  • Analytics data — through Google Analytics, including pages visited, approximate location, device type, and how you navigate the site. This helps us understand which tools are useful and where to improve.
  • Technical and log data — our hosting provider may automatically record information such as your IP address, browser type, and access times as part of normal server operation and security.

4. The Currency Converter tool

The Currency Converter is the one tool on ConvertKoro that contacts an external service by design: it fetches current exchange rates from a free, public exchange-rate API (open.er-api.com) each time you load the page. Only a generic rate request is sent — no amount you type, no currency you select, and no personal information is included in that request.

5. Cookies and advertising (Google AdSense)

This website uses Google AdSense, an advertising service provided by Google, to display ads. To do this, Google and its partners use cookies and similar technologies:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website and other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the Internet.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings.

For more on how Google uses this data, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

6. Your choices and opt-out options

  • Manage Google's ad personalization at Google Ads Settings.
  • Opt out of personalized ads from participating vendors at aboutads.info/choices.
  • Visitors in Europe can use youronlinechoices.eu.
  • You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time.

7. Consent (EEA, UK, and Switzerland)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you will be shown a consent message before personalized ads and non-essential cookies are used. You may grant, decline, or change your choices at any time. Withdrawing consent does not affect data already processed.

8. Third-party services

  • Google AdSense — advertising. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Analytics — site usage statistics. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Netlify — website hosting and delivery. See Netlify's Privacy Policy.
  • open.er-api.com — provides live exchange rates for the Currency Converter tool only.
  • CDN-hosted libraries (cdnjs, unpkg) — deliver the JavaScript and processing engines that power each tool, including the OCR language files and the video-processing engine.

9. Data security

Because your files are never transmitted to us, they are not exposed to interception in transit or storage on our side. The website is served over a secure HTTPS connection.

10. Children's privacy

This Service is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. International visitors

The Service is available globally. Automatically collected data described above may be processed in countries other than your own, per the policies of the third-party providers listed above.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, particularly as new tools are added to the Service. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes.
