Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026

In short: Your files are processed entirely inside your own browser. They are never uploaded to, transmitted to, or stored on any server. The data described below relates only to advertising and standard website hosting.

1. Who we are

ConvertKoro ("we," "us," or "the Service") operates a set of free PDF and image tools, currently hosted at convertkoro.netlify.app. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use the Service and how it is handled.

2. Your files are not collected

Every tool — PDF Merger, PDF Compressor, Image to PDF, PDF to Image, JPG to PDF, PDF Splitter, QR Code Generator, and Image to Text — runs locally in your web browser using client-side code (including the pdf-lib, pdf.js, and Tesseract.js libraries). This means:

  • Your files never leave your device.
  • We do not upload, receive, view, store, or retain any documents or images you process.
  • Once you close or reload the page, any files held in browser memory are discarded.

Because of this design, we have no access to the contents of your files at any point. The Image to Text tool downloads a small OCR language file from a public CDN the first time you use it; this file contains no data about you or your image.

3. Information collected automatically

While the tools themselves do not collect your files, some standard information is collected automatically when you visit the site, primarily to display advertising and to operate and secure the website:

  • Advertising data — through Google AdSense (see Section 4), including cookies and similar identifiers, your approximate location, device and browser information, and how you interact with ads.
  • Technical and log data — our hosting provider may automatically record information such as your IP address, browser type, and access times as part of normal server operation and security.

4. Cookies and advertising (Google AdSense)

This website uses Google AdSense, an advertising service provided by Google, to display ads. To do this, Google and its partners use cookies and similar technologies:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website and other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the Internet.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings.

For more on how Google uses this data, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

5. Your choices and opt-out options

  • Manage Google's ad personalization at Google Ads Settings.
  • Opt out of personalized ads from participating vendors at aboutads.info/choices.
  • Visitors in Europe can use youronlinechoices.eu.
  • You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time.

6. Consent (EEA, UK, and Switzerland)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you will be shown a consent message before personalized ads and non-essential cookies are used. You may grant, decline, or change your choices at any time. Withdrawing consent does not affect data already processed.

7. Third-party services

  • Google AdSense — advertising. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Netlify — website hosting and delivery. See Netlify's Privacy Policy.
  • CDN-hosted libraries (cdnjs) — deliver the JavaScript that powers each tool, including the Image to Text language file.

8. Data security

Because your files are never transmitted to us, they are not exposed to interception in transit or storage on our side. The website is served over a secure HTTPS connection.

9. Children's privacy

This Service is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

The Service is available globally. Automatically collected data described above may be processed in countries other than your own, per the policies of the third-party providers listed above.
