FAQ

Do these QR codes expire?

No. Since the content is encoded directly into the code's pattern, it works for as long as the image exists — there's no server or account behind it to expire.

Can I track scans?

Not with this tool, since there's no server involved. If you need scan analytics, you'd need a link-shortener service that logs clicks.

What can I put in the code?

Any text works — a website link, Wi-Fi details formatted as text, a phone number, or a short message.
