FAQ

What metadata gets removed?

GPS location, camera make/model, timestamps, and other EXIF data embedded by your phone or camera.

Does this change how the photo looks?

No — the image looks identical. Only the hidden metadata is stripped.

How do I know if my photo has location data in it?

Most phone photos do by default, unless location tagging is turned off in your camera app. This tool doesn't show you what was found — it just strips everything, so you don't need to check first.

Does this work on Mac, Windows, or my phone?

Yes — it runs in any modern browser on Mac, Windows, Chromebook, iPhone, or Android. No app or software to install.
