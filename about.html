About ConvertKoro

Real tools, doing one job well, without asking for your files first.

Why it exists

Most free converter sites work the same way: you upload your file to their server, wait, then download the result. That's a real, deliberate exchange — your document, in exchange for the free service. ConvertKoro exists because that exchange isn't actually necessary for most of these tasks. A browser can merge PDFs, resize an image, or convert a file format entirely on its own, with no server involved at all. So that's what most of these tools do.

How it works

Merging, compressing, converting, splitting, cropping, editing — the processing happens as code running in your own browser tab. Your file never leaves your device for most tools here, because it never needs to. There are two honest exceptions, and they're disclosed exactly where you'd expect: the URL Shortener stores the destination link you submit, because that's the only way a short link can redirect anyone; and Image-to-Text sends your image to a cloud OCR service by default for meaningfully better accuracy, with an on-device-only option available if you'd rather it stayed local. Every other tool stays fully client-side, and where it doesn't, we say so.

What's here

Tools live so far across PDF, image, media, developer, and calculator utilities. Each one is free, has no daily limit, and doesn't require an account — drop a file in, get your result, done.

How it's free

A small amount of advertising on the page supports the cost of running the site. There's no premium tier, no paywall, and no feature held back behind a subscription.

Who's behind it

ConvertKoro is an independent project, not a company — a small, growing team built it, starting from one developer who got tired of "free" tools that quietly needed your files uploaded first, and built the alternative. Testing and feedback now come from a second person too, and the team is expected to grow further as the site does.
