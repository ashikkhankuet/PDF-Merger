About ConvertKoro

A small toolkit, built with one rule.

The rule

Your files stay on your device. Every tool on this site — merging, compressing, converting, splitting — runs as code in your browser. There's no server in the middle receiving your documents, which means there's nothing for us to store, lose, or hand over. We built ConvertKoro because most free converters ask you to upload your files first and trust a stranger's server with them. This one doesn't ask.

What's here

Nine tools so far: PDF Merger, PDF Compressor, Image to PDF, PDF to Image, JPG to PDF, PDF to Word (text-only), PDF Splitter, QR Code Generator, and Image to Text. Each one is free, has no daily limit, and doesn't require an account. More tools get added as we build them.

How it's free

ConvertKoro is supported by a small amount of advertising on the page. There's no premium tier, no paywall, and no feature held back behind a subscription.

Who's behind it

ConvertKoro is an independent project, built and maintained by a small team who wanted a converter site that didn't feel like a data-collection funnel.
