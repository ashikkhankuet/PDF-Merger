FAQ

Is this guaranteed to be accepted?

No tool can guarantee acceptance — this prepares your photo according to the selected country's published size and background requirements, but the issuing authority makes the final decision. Passport photo rules can change, so always confirm current requirements with the issuing authority before submitting.

Where do these size requirements come from?

Each country's requirements link to their real, official source — shown directly on this page. Where official sources didn't specify every detail, that's disclosed rather than guessed.

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

No — face detection and background processing both run entirely in your browser using the same on-device engine as Background Remover. Your photo is never sent to a server.

Can I get a printable sheet with multiple copies?

Yes — after creating your photo, choose a 4×6" print sheet with 2, 4, 6, or 8 copies laid out for one print.

Does this work on Mac, Windows, or my phone?

Yes — it runs in any modern browser on Mac, Windows, Chromebook, iPhone, or Android. No app or software to install.
