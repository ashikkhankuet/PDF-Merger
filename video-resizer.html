FAQ

Will resizing affect video quality?

Shrinking generally looks sharp. Enlarging beyond the original resolution can look softer since no new detail is created.

Can I resize to a specific aspect ratio like 16:9?

Yes — uncheck "Keep aspect ratio" and enter your exact target width and height.

Does this work on Mac, Windows, or my phone?

Yes — it runs in any modern browser on Mac, Windows, Chromebook, iPhone, or Android. No app or software to install.
