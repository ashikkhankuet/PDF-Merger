FAQ

Why is the output always WAV?

WAV keeps this tool fast and dependency-free. Run the result through the Audio Converter afterward if you need MP3.

What audio formats can I trim?

Any format your browser can play — MP3, WAV, M4A, OGG, and most common audio files.

Does this work on Mac, Windows, or my phone?

Yes — it runs in any modern browser on Mac, Windows, Chromebook, iPhone, or Android. No app or software to install.
