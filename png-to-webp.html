FAQ

Does converting PNG to WebP keep transparency?

Yes — WebP supports transparency just like PNG, so transparent areas are preserved.

How much smaller will the file be?

It varies by image, but WebP is typically noticeably smaller than PNG at equivalent quality.

Does this work on Mac, Windows, or my phone?
