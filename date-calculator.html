FAQ

How do I find out how many days are between two dates?

Switch to "Days between two dates," enter a start and end date, and the total days, weeks, weekdays, and weekend days appear instantly.

Can I calculate a date 90 days from today?

Yes — switch to "Add / subtract days," set the start date to today, enter 90 in Amount, choose Days, and Add.

Does this account for leap years?

Yes, all calculations use standard JavaScript date arithmetic, which correctly accounts for leap years and varying month lengths.
