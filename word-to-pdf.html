FAQ

Will my document’s exact formatting be preserved?

Headings, paragraphs, and lists come through cleanly. Tables, images, and precise page layout are not preserved.

Does this work with .doc files, or only .docx?

Only .docx (the modern Word format). Older .doc files aren’t supported.
