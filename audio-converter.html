FAQ

What formats can I convert from?

MP3, WAV, M4A, OGG, FLAC, AAC, and WMA all convert reliably — a real audio engine runs in your browser, not just native browser decoding.

Why choose WAV over MP3?

WAV is lossless and instant to produce, but larger. MP3 is much smaller and takes a bit longer to encode, since it runs a real MP3 encoder in your browser.

Does this work on Mac, Windows, or my phone?

Yes — it runs in any modern browser on Mac, Windows, Chromebook, iPhone, or Android. No app or software to install.
