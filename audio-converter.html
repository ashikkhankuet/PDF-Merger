FAQ

What formats can I convert from?

Anything your browser can decode — MP3, WAV, M4A, OGG, and most common formats.

Why choose WAV over MP3?

WAV is lossless and instant to produce, but larger. MP3 is much smaller and takes a bit longer to encode, since it runs a real MP3 encoder in your browser.
