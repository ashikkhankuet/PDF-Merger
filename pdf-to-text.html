When you just need the words out of a PDF — for a search, a quote, or pasting into another document — extracting plain text is faster than opening a full PDF viewer. This reads real, selectable text directly from the file, entirely in your browser, with nothing uploaded and no account needed.

FAQ

My PDF is a scan — nothing came out?

Scanned PDFs are images of text, not real characters, so there's nothing here to extract. Use PDF OCR instead, or Image to Text for individual pages.

Can I extract text from a password-protected PDF?

Not currently — password-protected or encrypted PDFs aren't supported. Remove the password first, then try again.

Does this work on Mac, Windows, or my phone?

Yes — it runs in any modern browser on Mac, Windows, Chromebook, iPhone, or Android. No app or software to install.
