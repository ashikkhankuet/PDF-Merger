FAQ

How many JPGs can I combine?

No fixed limit — add as many as you need, limited only by your device's memory for very large batches.

Can I include PNG files too?

This page accepts JPG only. For a mix of formats, use the Image to PDF tool.

Will photo quality be reduced?

No — each photo is embedded at its original resolution.

Does this work on Mac, Windows, or my phone?

Yes — it runs in any modern browser on Mac, Windows, Chromebook, iPhone, or Android. No app or software to install.
