FAQ

What kind of photos does this work best on?

This tool is optimized for photos of people — portraits, passport photos, profile pictures — using Google's selfie-segmentation model. It can work on other subjects too, but people and clear single subjects give the most reliable results.

What background options are there?

Transparent (PNG), White, Black, Blue, Red, or any custom color you pick — common choices for passport photos, ID cards, and professional profile pictures.

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

No — the background removal model runs entirely in your browser using WebAssembly. Your photo is never sent to a server.

Why do the edges look a little rough on hair or fine details?

Automatic background removal is genuinely harder on fine details like flyaway hair or fur — this is a real, known limitation of every automatic background remover, not just this one. For passport-style headshots against a plain background, results are typically clean.

Does this work on Mac, Windows, or my phone?

Yes — it runs in any modern browser on Mac, Windows, Chromebook, iPhone, or Android. No app or software to install.
