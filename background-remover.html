How it works

  1. Add your photoA clear photo with the subject reasonably visible works best.
  2. Click Remove backgroundAI detects the subject on our server and removes the background.
  3. Pick a backgroundTransparent, a preset color, or your own custom color.

Good to know

  • Optimized for people — portraits, passport photos, profile pictures give the most reliable results
  • Transparent background downloads as a PNG; any solid color also downloads as PNG
  • Fine details like flyaway hair are genuinely harder for any automatic tool — a real, common limitation, not unique to this one
  • Photos up to 4MB \u2014 larger photos should be resized first

FAQ

What kind of photos does this work best on?

This tool works well on most clear, well-lit photos with a single main subject — people, portraits, passport photos, and profile pictures give the most reliable results.

What background options are there?

Transparent (PNG), White, Black, Blue, Red, or any custom color you pick — common choices for passport photos, ID cards, and professional profile pictures.

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

Your photo is sent to our server for real AI-based processing, then deleted afterward. The result is composited with your chosen background right in your browser.

Why do the edges look a little rough on hair or fine details?

Automatic background removal is genuinely harder on fine details like flyaway hair or fur — this is a real, known limitation of every automatic background remover, not just this one. For passport-style headshots against a plain background, results are typically clean.

Does this work on Mac, Windows, or my phone?

Yes — it runs in any modern browser on Mac, Windows, Chromebook, iPhone, or Android. No app or software to install.
