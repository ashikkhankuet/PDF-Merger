PDF to Word

Pulls the readable text out of a PDF and packages it into a real, editable .docx file.

This is a text-only conversion. It extracts the words from your PDF into an editable Word document, but does not reproduce the original layout, images, tables, or exact formatting. A full layout-preserving conversion would require a specialized server-side engine — this stays free, local, and private by keeping to text.

FAQ

Why doesn't this keep my original formatting?

True layout-preserving PDF-to-Word conversion needs specialized software that rebuilds fonts, positioning, and tables — something that isn't reliably possible in browser JavaScript. This tool focuses on getting your text out cleanly and editably instead.

My PDF is a scan — why did nothing come out?

Scanned PDFs are actually images of text, not real text characters, so there's nothing for this tool to extract. Use Image to Text for scanned pages instead.

Can I edit the result in Word?

Yes — the output is a standard .docx file, fully editable in Microsoft Word, Google Docs, or LibreOffice.

Can I convert a password-protected PDF?

Not currently — password-protected or encrypted PDFs aren't supported. Remove the password first, then convert.

Does this work on Mac, Windows, or my phone?

Yes — it runs in any modern browser on Mac, Windows, Chromebook, iPhone, or Android. No app or software to install.
