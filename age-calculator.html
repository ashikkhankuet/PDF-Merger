Calculating someone's exact age — in years, months, and days, not just a year subtraction — is needed for forms, eligibility checks, or just curiosity. This also shows total time lived and a countdown to the next birthday.

How it works

  1. Enter the date of birthPick it from the calendar or type it in.
  2. Choose the "as of" dateDefaults to today, or pick any date to calculate age at that point.
  3. Read the full breakdownExact age, total time lived in several units, and a countdown to the next birthday.

FAQ

How is age calculated exactly?

By comparing your date of birth to the "as of" date down to the day, giving a precise years/months/days breakdown rather than just subtracting birth year from the current year.

Can I calculate someone else's age, or age on a future date?

Yes — enter any date of birth and any "as of" date, past or future.
