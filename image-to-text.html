How it works

  1. Add your imageA clear photo, scan, or screenshot works best.
  2. Run text extractionOn-device OCR reads the image and pulls out the text it finds.
  3. Copy the resultEdit directly in the box, or copy it straight into another document.

Good to know

  • Works best on clear, well-lit, horizontal text
  • Handwriting recognition is far less reliable than printed text
  • Stylized, embossed, or decorative fonts (like on cards or signage) are harder to read accurately than plain printed text
  • Dense tables with many small columns, or colored/highlighted table cells, are the hardest case for this tool — expect occasional row misalignment or character mistakes on this kind of content specifically
  • Pick the right language above for non-English text — English is selected by default and won't read other scripts correctly
  • First run in a language downloads a small language file, then caches it in your browser

FAQ

Why is the text extraction not perfect?

OCR accuracy depends heavily on image clarity, lighting, and font. Blurry, angled, or low-contrast photos will produce more errors than a clean scan.

Does this work on handwriting?

It can attempt handwriting, but accuracy is much lower than for printed or typed text.

Is my image sent anywhere for processing?

By default, no — the OCR engine runs entirely in your browser and your image is never uploaded. If you tap "Try enhanced accuracy" after a result, that specific image is sent to a cloud OCR service for a second, stronger attempt — this is always your choice, never automatic.

Can this read Bangla (Bengali) text?

Yes — Bengali and English are both enabled by default, so photos or screenshots with Bangla text are read automatically without changing any setting.

Does this work on Mac, Windows, or my phone?

Yes — it runs in any modern browser on Mac, Windows, Chromebook, iPhone, or Android. No app or software to install.
