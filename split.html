How it works

  1. Add your PDFDrop in the file you want to split or extract pages from.
  2. Choose a modeExtract a specific range like "1-3, 5", or split every page into its own file.
  3. Split and downloadA single extract downloads as one PDF; splitting every page downloads as a ZIP.

Good to know

  • Use commas for individual pages and dashes for ranges: 1-3, 5, 8-10
  • Page numbers start at 1
  • Original page quality is preserved exactly

FAQ

How do I extract just pages 2 and 4?

Type 2, 4 into the pages field, choose "Extract page range," and download — you'll get a single PDF with just those two pages, in order.

What does "Split every page" do?

It creates a separate one-page PDF for every page in the document, then bundles them into a downloadable ZIP.

Can I reorder pages while splitting?

Not on this page — pages keep their original order. Extract the ones you want, then use PDF Merger to combine them in a new order.
