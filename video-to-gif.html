FAQ

Why keep GIF clips short?

GIFs don’t compress as efficiently as video — a 5–10 second clip keeps the file size reasonable.

Can I choose which part of the video becomes the GIF?

Yes — set the start time and duration to pick exactly the clip you want.

Does this work on Mac, Windows, or my phone?

Yes — it runs in any modern browser on Mac, Windows, Chromebook, iPhone, or Android. No app or software to install.
