FAQ

Why does conversion take a moment?

HEIC decoding runs a WebAssembly library entirely in your browser tab — larger photos can take a few seconds, especially on older devices.

How it works

  1. Add your HEIC photoDrop an iPhone HEIC/HEIF file in, or click to browse.
  2. Let it decodeA WebAssembly decoder runs in your tab — no upload, just a few seconds of processing.
  3. Download the JPGA standard JPG downloads straight to your device.
