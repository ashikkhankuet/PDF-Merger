iPhone photos save as HEIC by default, a format many apps, websites, and older devices can't open. Converting to JPG makes those photos usable everywhere — for uploading to a website, attaching to an email, or sharing with someone on Android or Windows. Conversion happens on your device, with nothing uploaded.

How it works

  1. Add your HEIC photoDrop an iPhone HEIC/HEIF file in, or click to browse.
  2. Let it decodeA WebAssembly decoder runs in your tab — no upload, just a few seconds of processing.
  3. Download the JPGA standard JPG downloads straight to your device.

FAQ

Why does conversion take a moment?

HEIC decoding runs a WebAssembly library entirely in your browser tab — larger photos can take a few seconds, especially on older devices.

Can I convert multiple HEIC photos at once?

This page converts one photo at a time. For several photos, repeat the process for each — everything still stays on your device.

Does this work on Mac, Windows, or my phone?

Yes — it runs in any modern browser on Mac, Windows, Chromebook, iPhone, or Android. No app or software to install.
